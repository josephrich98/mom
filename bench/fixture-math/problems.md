## Worked examples

A teaching assistant drafted these examples quickly, and they have not yet been checked.

### Example 1

Differentiate $f(x) = x^3 \ln x$ and evaluate $f'(e)$.

By the product rule, $f'(x) = 3x^2 \ln x + x^3$. Therefore $f'(e) = 3e^2 + e^3 \approx 42.252$.

### Example 2

Evaluate $\int_1^3 (2x + 1)\,dx$.

An antiderivative is $x^2 + x$, so the integral is $(9 + 3) - (1 + 1) = 12 - 1 = 11$.

### Example 3

Decide whether $\mathbf{M} = \begin{pmatrix} 2 & 1 \\ 5 & 3 \end{pmatrix}$ is invertible.

The determinant is $2 \cdot 3 + 1 \cdot 5 = 11$. Because the determinant is nonzero, $\mathbf{M}$ is invertible.
